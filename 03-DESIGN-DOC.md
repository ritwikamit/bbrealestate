# Baba Baidyanath Real Estate --- Design Document

## Design goal

Create a premium, trustworthy Indian real-estate corporate identity
without pretending that the company already has a large property
portfolio.

Keywords: **Trust · Long-term value · Quality · Professionalism · Local
presence**

## Visual style

Modern Indian real-estate / corporate luxury.

Avoid: - Cheap property templates - Excessive gold - Ornate decoration -
Generic skyscraper imagery - Fake luxury claims - Heavy gradients

Use: - Architectural photography when supplied - Large whitespace -
Strong typography - Fine borders - Warm neutrals - Deep green -
Charcoal - Restrained brass/gold

## Palette

``` css
--charcoal: #151716;
--ivory: #F5F2EA;
--stone: #D9D4C8;
--green: #4E5B45;
--green-deep: #29352D;
--gold: #B8944A;
--white: #FFFFFF;
--muted: #6E716B;
```

Approximate balance: 45% charcoal/green, 40% ivory/white, 8% stone, 7%
gold.

## Typography

Headings: Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display / DM Serif Display.
Body: Inter / Manrope / Geist.

## Navigation

Logo/wordmark About \| Services \| Projects \| Contact CTA: **ENQUIRE
NOW**

Because no verified official logo was found in the researched sources,
do not invent an official corporate logo. Use a refined text wordmark
until brand assets are supplied.

## Hero

Suggested: **BUILDING VALUE. CREATING PLACES.**

Supporting: A modern real-estate company based in Aurangabad, Bihar.

CTA: ENQUIRE NOW Secondary: ABOUT THE COMPANY

Use an architectural image only when a legitimate company-approved image
is supplied.

## Corporate facts

Present: 2024 --- Incorporated ACTIVE --- Status ROC PATNA AURANGABAD
--- BIHAR

CIN may be displayed in the corporate information section.

## About

Editorial layout with concise factual copy. Do not claim market
leadership, years of experience or completed developments without
evidence.

## Projects

If no approved projects exist: **PROJECT PORTFOLIO** "Project
information will be presented here as developments and property
opportunities are made available."

## CTA

**LET'S DISCUSS YOUR PROPERTY REQUIREMENT.**

## Footer

Company name, CIN, registered address, About, Services, Projects,
Contact, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer.

## Motion

Subtle image reveal, text fade, gold-line expansion and restrained hover
effects. Respect reduced motion.

## Design principle

The site should look established and professional while remaining honest
about the limited public information currently available.
