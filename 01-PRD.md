## Corporate facts

Use verified facts such as incorporation date, active status, CIN and
ROC. Do not turn third-party directory data into unsupported marketing
claims.

## Projects

Do not invent projects, buildings, prices, amenities, unit sizes,
possession dates, RERA numbers or returns. If no approved projects are
available, use a professional "Project portfolio coming soon" state.

## Services

The information architecture should support property development, sales,
acquisition, investment/advisory, leasing and land opportunities, but
these are potential categories---not verified offerings. Publish only
company-approved services.
